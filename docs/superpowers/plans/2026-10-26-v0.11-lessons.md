1. **A `recorded: plate` slide with no plate** (never recorded, or every URL 404s): the live world runs there, not the living still and not black. → Task 4, `layerNow` test "a recorded slide whose plate is missing runs the world".
2. **Long frames that are not slowness:** a tab coming back from hidden, the world resuming after a clip covered it, the first seconds of shader compiles, a recorder run (`__stageMaxDt`). None may trip the watchdog. → Task 5, tests "a resume gap is not a slow frame", "grace after start and after reset".
3. **No music:** `musicClock()` before the first gesture (suspended context), with the track paused, ended or never started; a `clock: music` slide then runs on wall time, and the world does not jump when the music starts. → Task 3, tests "no clock without a playing track", "stepReal caps a jump".
4. **Re-entering a slide:** a back-step into a slide mid-collision, or a jump past it: the detector's event times are cleared, `steady` is the new slide's, StageSteps' later-click steps apply. → Task 1 "a latch stopped and faded is all OFF", "a restart during the fade"; Task 2 "entered again after leaving".
5. **The presenter window and print:** no music plays in the presenter window, the watchdog does not rebuild a print page's or presenter's world into a plate, the slide hooks do nothing in previews. → Task 3 wiring test "StageSoundtrack plays only in the audience window"; Task 5 wiring test "no watchdog under print/presenter".

  - Stage.vue: on a recorded-plate slide the world pauses once the plate is `playing` or `held` and wakes on leaving; a `sync` runs a rAF loop that reads `musicClock(sync.track)` and the slide's video (the plate's current `<video>`, or for `clip` the current slide's `.video-player video`) and applies `slaveRate`.
